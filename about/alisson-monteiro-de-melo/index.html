Alisson Monteiro de Melo: The Entrepreneurial Vision Behind Twelve Cleaning

From early ventures in Rio de Janeiro to a new vision of home care in Georgia, a journey shaped by entrepreneurship, operational innovation, theological studies, and family life.

From his first business experiences in Rio de Janeiro to the development of Twelve Cleaning in Georgia, Alisson Monteiro de Melo’s story connects entrepreneurship, operational innovation, theological studies, and a belief in the importance of family life.

An Entrepreneurial Journey That Began at Twelve

Long before establishing Twelve Cleaning in Georgia, Alisson Monteiro de Melo began exploring entrepreneurship in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil.

His first business experience dates to 2013, when, at the age of 12, he started a small electronics repair operation in his family's garage, servicing computers, mobile phones, and tablets.

What began as a modest garage-based venture gradually developed into a more structured business. By the age of 16, Monteiro de Melo had expanded the operation into his first physical retail store.

These early experiences introduced him to the practical challenges of customer service, business organization, and managing a growing operation.

In 2019, at the age of 18, he founded Rabinos Sorvete, an ice cream business in Rio de Janeiro.

According to Monteiro de Melo, the business grew from a single location in 2019 to 12 stores by 2021.

For him, this period reinforced an important entrepreneurial principle: expanding a business requires more than an attractive product or a promising idea. It requires repeatable processes, disciplined execution, and the ability to organize people around a consistent operating model.

These lessons would later influence his contributions to Marcelo Decorações and the founding philosophy behind Twelve Cleaning.

Transforming Operations at Marcelo Decorações

Another significant chapter in his entrepreneurial development took place at Marcelo Decorações, his family’s custom furniture business in Brazil.

Working in an advisory capacity, Monteiro de Melo contributed to initiatives focused on management processes, recruitment, team organization, production workflows, and customer delivery procedures.

One of the operational improvements he describes involved preparing furniture components for delivery in an organized, packaged format, allowing installation teams to work with greater consistency at customers’ homes.

This approach reflected his interest in solving practical business problems through repeatable processes.

According to company figures provided by Monteiro de Melo, monthly revenue increased from approximately R$56,000 to R$280,000 over a period of roughly two and a half years, during which operational and management changes were introduced.

The experience reinforced a principle that would later influence the development of Twelve Cleaning: the quality of a service depends not only on the final result, but also on the systems and people responsible for delivering it.

When Theology and Entrepreneurship Met

After moving to the United States, Monteiro de Melo pursued theological studies.

During his academic journey, he became particularly interested in the relationship between family life, responsibility, and the environment in which people live.

His reflections on theology and the family led him to consider a question connecting his academic interests with his entrepreneurial background:

“How does the environment of a home influence the everyday experience of the people who live there?”

A home is where families build routines, raise children, rest after demanding days, and share some of their most meaningful experiences.

For Monteiro de Melo, the physical condition of that environment became part of a broader reflection on family well-being.

Scientific research provides one perspective on the subject.

Researchers Stephanie McMains and Sabine Kastner, affiliated with Princeton University, examined how competing visual stimuli interact with the brain’s mechanisms of attention.

Their findings contribute to an understanding of how the human visual system processes complex environments.

This research does not establish that professional cleaning directly improves mental health or family relationships. However, it provides a scientific perspective on visual environments and attention.

Monteiro de Melo’s interest developed at the intersection of these subjects: family life, environmental organization, operational systems, and the delivery of reliable services.

The Creation of Twelve Cleaning in Georgia

Founded in Georgia in 2024, Twelve Cleaning emerged from a vision of professional home care centered on consistency, trust, and attention to detail.

The company’s approach reflects an effort to apply structured business processes to a highly personal service: caring for the spaces where people live.

Rather than treating residential cleaning as a series of disconnected tasks, Twelve Cleaning emphasizes organized service procedures, customer communication, accountability, and long-term relationships.

Its guiding statement summarizes that philosophy:

“We don’t just clean. We bring peace of mind.”

The company’s daily operations are led by Ruth, its CEO, who oversees service execution and operational management.

Within its first years, Twelve Cleaning developed a recurring customer base in the Atlanta metropolitan area, serving households seeking dependable residential cleaning services.

According to company-provided projections, Twelve Cleaning has set a revenue forecast of approximately $1.5 million for 2026 and a projected revenue target of $1.8 million for 2027. Both figures are forward-looking estimates, not achieved or independently audited revenue.

These figures are company-reported and should be understood within the context of Twelve Cleaning’s ongoing business development.

The Signature Experience

The Signature Plan: Turning a Philosophy Into a Home Care Experience

One of the distinctive concepts developed through the founding vision of Twelve Cleaning is the Signature Plan, a recurring home cleaning service designed around a broader understanding of what it means to care for a home.

For Alisson Monteiro de Melo, the concept emerged from a simple observation: families do not necessarily seek professional cleaning only because their homes need attention. They also want to spend less time worrying about household responsibilities and enjoy living spaces that feel organized, comfortable, and welcoming.

This perspective influenced the development of the Signature Plan.

Rather than focusing exclusively on visible cleanliness, the service emphasizes thoughtful attention to detail, consistency, and the overall presentation of the home.

The goal is to create an environment that feels cared for — one in which customers can return from a demanding day and experience a greater sense of order, comfort, and peace of mind.

“The experience of living in a well-maintained home matters just as much as the cleaning process itself.”

As one of the company’s recurring service options, Signature gives customers an opportunity to choose a more comprehensive approach to ongoing home care.

Its development illustrates how Monteiro de Melo’s interest in family life, environmental organization, and structured service systems became part of Twelve Cleaning’s business philosophy.

For the company, Signature is more than a service package. It represents an effort to translate its founding purpose into a consistent customer experience.

A Business Philosophy Rooted in Systems and People

Across food retail, furniture manufacturing, and residential services, Monteiro de Melo’s entrepreneurial experiences have shared a consistent theme: the importance of operational structure.

His approach emphasizes the relationship between management systems, service consistency, and the experiences of the people involved.

At Twelve Cleaning, that philosophy is reflected in the company’s commitment to clear service expectations, organized procedures, professional responsibility, and respect for customers’ homes.

The company’s development also reflects the importance of operational leadership, with Ruth directing the execution of its day-to-day services.

Together, these elements have shaped Twelve Cleaning’s identity as a Georgia-based home care company focused on building lasting relationships with the families it serves.

Beyond Cleaning: A Broader Vision of Home Care

For Monteiro de Melo, the story behind Twelve Cleaning is connected to a larger question about how businesses can create value in everyday life.

His experiences in Brazil introduced him to the challenges of business growth and operational management.

His studies in the United States expanded his interest in family life and the significance of the home.

Twelve Cleaning brought these interests together within a residential service business.

The company’s philosophy does not depend on the idea that cleaning alone can transform family life. Instead, it recognizes that dependable home care can be one practical way of supporting households as they manage their daily responsibilities.

As Twelve Cleaning looks toward the future, its focus remains on building consistent services, developing its teams, and strengthening the trust of the customers it serves.

For Monteiro de Melo, that is the connection between entrepreneurship and home care: thoughtful systems can help people deliver meaningful services with greater consistency.

The purpose behind Twelve Cleaning is not simply to clean homes. It is to care for the environments where everyday life happens.

“We don’t just clean. We bring peace of mind.”

About Alisson Monteiro de Melo

Alisson Monteiro de Melo is a Brazilian entrepreneur associated with the founding vision of Twelve Cleaning, a residential and commercial cleaning company established in Georgia in 2024.

His entrepreneurial background includes experiences in food retail and operational advisory work within the custom furniture industry in Brazil.

His interests include business management, operational systems, entrepreneurship, theology, and family life.
