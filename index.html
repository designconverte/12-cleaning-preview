How it works

One reset. Then a rhythm.

Every new home starts the same way, so every visit after it can be simpler.

  1. 1

    First visit

    Deep Cleaning Reset

    We remove the built-up dust, grease and missed areas, so future visits keep your home up instead of chasing old dirt.

  2. 2

    Then

    Your maintenance plan

    Weekly or every other week. The same checklist, the same standard, every visit. Your plan comes in two levels of detail; the higher one adds extra care on high-touch areas, baseboards and doors.

  3. 3

    Whenever you want

    Add-ons

    Inside the fridge, oven or cabinets, interior windows, organization and special projects. Ask, and we add them to your estimate.

Why this rhythm. We recommend weekly or every other week. Homes that go more than 30 days between visits build up again, and the next visit needs extra time.

No surprise on price. If your home needs more time than the details suggested, we tell you before we start.

Trust, explained

Letting someone into your home is a big decision. Here's how it works.

  1. When we arrive

    Visits are booked with an arrival window rather than an exact time.

  2. How we get in

    You don't need to be home. Leave a door code, a key, garage access or simple instructions. We lock up when we leave.

  3. Who comes in

    Every team member passes a background check and receives professional training before entering your home.

  4. The same team

    The same team comes back to your home visit after visit, so they learn how you like it.

  5. Every visit

    One structured checklist, so the standard stays the same. We clean under, behind and into the corners.

  6. What we bring

    Professional-grade supplies and equipment. Prefer a product of your own? Leave it out and we'll use it.

  7. Your pets and your things

    Homes with pets are welcome. If a pet needs to stay in a room, please secure it before we arrive. Before the first visit, tell us about pets, alarms and anything we shouldn't touch.

  8. If something breaks

    We're fully insured. If anything is damaged, tell us within 24 hours, with photos when you can.

Questions

Good questions to ask before you let anyone in.

Before you book

Do I need to be home?

No. Give us a door code, a key, garage access or simple instructions, and the team locks up when they leave. Please don't type codes into the form; we set up access with you directly.

Do you bring the supplies?

Yes. We bring professional-grade supplies and equipment. If you'd like us to use a specific product, leave it out and tell us.

How long does a cleaning take?

Most home cleanings take 2 to 4 hours, depending on the home and the service. For businesses, it depends on the scope and how often we come.

What's the difference between regular and deep cleaning?

Regular cleaning maintains your home. Deep cleaning adds detail: baseboards, doors, deeper bathroom work and the areas that usually get skipped. That's why most homes start with a Deep Cleaning Reset.

Are you insured?

Yes. Twelve Cleaning is fully insured, and every visit follows the same structured protocol.

Do you offer recurring cleaning?

Yes, and it's what we're built for. Homes are on a weekly, every-other-week or custom schedule. Business schedules run from weekly to daily.

How do I get an estimate?

Use the form at the top of this page. It takes less than 2 minutes, and the estimate is free. For businesses, we usually start with a quick walkthrough. You can also call (470) 265-6367.

What should I prepare before the first cleaning?

Three things: how we get in, the areas that matter most to you, and notes on pets, alarms or products. Switching from another company? We may recommend starting with a Deep Cleaning Reset.

Do you clean inside the oven, fridge or cabinets?

Yes, as add-ons. Ask for them in the form, and we'll include them in your estimate.

Which areas do you serve?

Homes in Alpharetta, Milton, Johns Creek, North Roswell and selected areas of Buckhead. Businesses across Greater Atlanta, including Sandy Springs, Midtown, Dunwoody, Brookhaven, Roswell and Marietta. Not sure about your ZIP? Send it in the form.

Policies, in plain English

Can the price change after my estimate?

Your estimate is a starting point. The final price may be adjusted for the home's actual condition, buildup, clutter or pet hair. If it differs from what you described, we share the updated total before we start, and you choose: approve, decline, reschedule or change the service.

What if I skip a visit or wait longer between cleanings?

Skipping, postponing or canceling a recurring visit may add a Skipped Service Adjustment to the next visit, because that clean takes extra work. Homes that go more than 30 days between visits may need extra time and a price adjustment.

What if something gets damaged?

Tell us within 24 hours, with photos or other documentation when possible. Twelve Cleaning is fully insured, and claims may be resolved by repair, replacement, reimbursement or insurance review. Limits are in our Terms.

What time will the team arrive?

We work with arrival windows, not exact times. Please make sure we can get in at the scheduled time; if the team can't access the home, a lockout or cancellation charge may apply.

When do I pay?

On the day of the service, unless we agree otherwise in writing. We accept cash, check, card, Zelle and ACH. Balances unpaid after 24 hours may incur a late fee.

Are pets OK?

Yes. Please secure pets when needed during the visit. We can't be responsible for a pet that gets out through a door or gate left unsecured.

What don't you clean?

Exterior and non-air-conditioned spaces, like garages, patios, balconies and unfinished basements, unless we add them in writing. We don't take on biohazards, excessive mold, infestations, animal waste or hoarding. Some conditions, like permanent stains, hard-water buildup or discolored grout, may not come out with standard cleaning.

Do you take photos in my home?

We may take before-and-after photos for internal quality control, and they never identify you. Prefer we don't? Tell us in advance, any time.
